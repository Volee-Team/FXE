## Rejected

- **Hiding a late cancel the way a Remove is hidden.** She asked for the
  opposite: the name stays, flagged, until she decides.
- **Waive as a confirmation dialog.** One tap with Undo beside "Fee waived"
  is faster courtside and a mis-tap costs one more tap.
- **Counting a Pool departure as something other than a cancel.** She signed
  up and withdrew, which is what she asked to see. If she wants spots only,
  that needs a new column recording where a cancel came from.
- **Exempting pros on the server too.** Pros do not register for clinics; if
  one does, the server still asks for the waiver and a card as for any player.
