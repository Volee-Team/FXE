## Rejected

- **Enforcing "card on file" while payments are off.** It would lock every
  tester out of registering until Stripe is connected, and the enforcement
  already exists for the moment it is.
- **Listing every upcoming clinic on Home.** Up to five weeks of rows; "open
  for registration" is what a player can act on and matches her empty line.
- **Keeping Home's View All Clinics as a quieter link.** Kat asked for one
  button or none; history lives on Profile now.
- **Deleting the admin unpaid reminder and Paid toggle.** They carry no Zelle
  words themselves and are unreachable behind `zelle_allowed`; hard rule 6.
- **Detecting a 105 by a new column.** The app already recognises a 105 by its
  name or category (the "?" explainer, 2026-08-16); a second definition would
  drift from the first.

## How we would know we were wrong

Tara or Kat looking at Home with one clinic and asking where the rest went, or
with three and asking why the open list vanished (question 59); a non-member
reporting they could not take a second 105 on a day that was not back to
back; a board asking for a number the report does not carry.
