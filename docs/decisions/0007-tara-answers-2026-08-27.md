# 0007 — Tara's answers on windows, juniors, tone and eligibility

**Date:** 2026-08-27 · **Status:** Active

## Decision

Six answers from Tara, each closing an open question in `docs/whats-next.md`.

### 1. Queen City eligibility is NOT enforced

> "Queen City does not have a pool. Too complicated to filter this, so let it go
> and I'll see who signs up."

No team-membership concept enters the schema. The clinic's description says who
it is for; anyone may register; Tara sees who signed up. This confirms the
assumption we were already shipping.

**One thing still ambiguous, deliberately not guessed:** "does not have a pool"
may mean she wants registrations on that clinic to go straight to **You're In!**
rather than into the Player Pool for her to select from. That would be a real
behavioural exception to hard rule 2, so it is asked rather than assumed. Until
she confirms, Queen City behaves like every other clinic.

### 2. Members get a 24-hour head start, same email, same schedule

> "Members get the same email 24 hours prior to nonmembers. Same exact email,
> same exact schedule. They just get first dibs for 24 hours"

**This confirms decision 0001 rather than changing it.** The rule already opens
members Thursday 8am and everyone else Friday 8am, which is exactly 24 hours,
and both tiers see one published schedule differing only in when they may
register. Pinned as a DURATION in `tests/sql/schema_decisions.sql`
(`tara_member_head_start_is_24h`) rather than as two timestamps, so the
assertion still reads as her sentence if the anchor day ever moves.

### 3. The NTRP rating guide stays as it is

> "Rating guide is good where it's at if it's volee material."

Closes `for-tara.md` question 7 and removes 13 strings from the copy-audit
backlog. `NTRPRating.swift` mirrors Volee verbatim on purpose so a player who
uses both apps reads identical words; that coupling is now hers, not ours, and
the note about changing it in both repos still applies.

### 4. First person in notifications is approved, with a tone bar

> "First person is ok. I want it to sound personal but not cheesy."

The three notifications that speak as Tara may stay in her voice. "Personal but
not cheesy" is now the standing bar for notification copy, and it is a real
constraint: it rules out exclamation-stacking and manufactured enthusiasm, which
is exactly the register AI drafts drift into. Hard rule 13 still applies, so new
copy is still hers or Alex-approved.

### 5. Registration closes 3 hours before the clinic

> "lets say 3 hours prior and if they try to register within 3 hours, they have
> the option to send me a direct message to get into the clinic, assuming there
> is space and it isn't full."

Implemented in `20260827000001` as `default_closes_at()`, a DEFAULT rather than
a constraint: she may set any close time per clinic, and this is what she gets
when she does not think about it.

The second half, a late-request path that messages her when the clinic is not
full, is a feature and is in `docs/backlog.md`. Closing registration without it
is strictly better than the previous state (it never closed at all, so clinics
that had already finished were still bookable) and strictly worse than her whole
answer, which is why the gap is written down rather than quietly deferred.

### 6. Juniors move to November or the spring session

> "Juniors we will figure out for November or spring session. No need to worry
> about it now"

Supersedes the worry raised in `docs/taras-real-week.md`, which flagged that her
own junior fall session starts 2026-08-24 and asked whether the app had to cover
it. It does not. Decision 0004 stands: juniors stay in the schema, unused, so
re-enabling is UI work rather than a migration against live data.

## Why this matters beyond the six answers

Her own framing:

> "I hate to say it but as much as we want this to be done in one shot, there
> will be some things that will need to be adjusted after trial and even during
> trial."

That is a design instruction, not an apology. Numbers she expects to move belong
in **one named place** so moving them is a one-line change rather than a hunt:
`default_closes_at()`, `member_opens_at()`, `public_opens_at()`,
`default_price_cents()`. Every one of those exists because a value she might
revise should never be scattered across call sites.

## Rejected

**Enforcing Queen City eligibility anyway, "to be safe".** It would add a team
roster concept, a private-clinic flag, and a whole permission surface, to solve a
problem she explicitly said not to solve. The cost of being wrong here is that
she declines someone by hand, which she already does.

**Treating the 3-hour close as settled.** It is a default with her warning
attached, not a rule. Writing it as a constraint would make her first adjustment
a migration.

## How we would know we were wrong

Players start messaging Tara asking why they cannot register for a clinic that
is clearly not full. That means 3 hours is too wide, or the late-request path is
overdue, and both are one change away by design.
