## What this decides

1. **No courtesy late cancellation.** Inside the cutoff the full fee applies,
   every time. `courtesy_cancel_days` becomes `0`, and `courtesy_available()`
   answers false whenever the window is zero, so the columns and the probe
   history stay and nothing is deleted (hard rule 4). The courtesy sentence,
   the "Late, courtesy used." notification suffix and the "Late · Courtesy"
   chip go.
2. **The cutoff is 3 hours**, which is also the registration close (decision
   0007 §5). A late cancellation is therefore exactly a cancellation after
   registration has closed. `cancel_cutoff_hours` becomes `3`; her policy
   text and both sentences change 4 to 3.
3. **The card is the only way to pay.** `zelle_allowed` becomes `false`.
   While it is false the Paid toggle, "Remind unpaid" and the Zelle reminder
   are not rendered on either admin surface. `payment_instructions` stays in
   the database (her exact string, decision 11) because the reminder RPC
   still composes from it, but nothing sends it. Until Stripe is live nothing
   tracks payment at all, which is what she asked for.
4. **The waiver is signed in the app.** Her document specifies the mechanism
   itself: a required checkbox, the participant's full legal name typed as an
   electronic signature, the email from the signed-in account, the time
   recorded by the app, and the version stored with the record. Built as
   `waivers` (versioned text) and `waiver_acceptances`; `register_for_clinic`
   refuses with `waiver_required` until the current version is accepted
   (admins exempt), and the app asks at sign-up and again whenever the RPC
   refuses.
5. **Account deletion keeps history.** A player's registrations, payments
   and Tara's notes stay; their name, phone, email, rating note and card
   summary are blanked; the sign-in is removed through Supabase's own admin
   API (soft delete) from an edge function, never by SQL against the auth
   schema.
6. **The level note shows in both places**: the roster row and the player's
   page. `search_players` returns it.
7. **Saturday and short weeks: confirmed as built** (decision 0001). She adds
   that there are no Saturday clinics, so the case is theoretical.
8. **Her tap charges; nothing automatic.** Confirms decision 0012 §4.
   Question 43 closed.

## Rejected

- **Dropping the courtesy columns and functions.** Hard rule 4 and decision
  history. A zero-day window switches the rule off without a destructive
  migration, and she may bring it back.
- **Deleting the auth user with a cascade.** `accounts.id` cascades from
  `auth.users`, and `players` and `registrations` cascade from there, so a
  hard delete would erase exactly the history she said to keep.
- **Removing the Paid toggle and reminder from the code.** Hard rule 6. They
  are gated on `zelle_allowed()`; flipping the setting brings them back.
