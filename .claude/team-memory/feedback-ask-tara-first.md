Alex, 2026-09-12: "remember help me get the info from TARA before we do things ALWAYS to make sure we're doing correct thing - make sure this is hard coded in you pls - always ask tara if you're unsure even 5%."

**Why:** Tara is the product owner and the only one who knows her policies (cancellation fees, windows, who pays what). Building on a guess costs a rebuild and her trust; asking costs a text message.

**How to apply:** Before starting work that encodes a business rule, price, policy, wording, or workflow of Tara's, write the exact questions (numbered, answerable in one line each) into `docs/questions-for-tara.md` and hand them to Alex to relay. Build only the parts that are policy-independent while waiting. Record her answers as a decision record with the date. This is also hard rule 14 in the repo's CLAUDE.md. Related: [[feedback-take-more-control]] (autonomy on engineering, not on her product decisions).

**Log every answer, the same turn (Alex, 2026-10-21: "shouldnt u be logging EVERY SINGLE TARA ANSWER ?????").** Her words verbatim into a dated decision record, the question's status line pointing at it. Her answers mostly come by text to Alex, which no tool here can read: before saying a question is open, search the chat log, the review page's saved answers and Alex's Gmail, then ask Alex to paste any reply, never assume none came.

**Write each question so it says what changes** (Alex, same day, on "'Waitlist' everywhere ...?": "this is confusing, you should say more like 'the word Waitlist is everywhere'"). Name the thing that changes and what it changes from: "Should the word 'Waitlist' replace 'Player Pool' everywhere in the app?" A one-word subject with a question mark makes her guess.
