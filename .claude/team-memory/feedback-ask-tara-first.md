**How to apply:** Before starting work that encodes a business rule, price, policy, wording, or workflow of Tara's, write the exact questions (numbered, answerable in one line each) into `docs/questions-for-tara.md` and hand them to Alex to relay. Build only the parts that are policy-independent while waiting. Record her answers as a decision record with the date. This is also hard rule 14 in the repo's CLAUDE.md.
