description: "Never suggest wrapping up, ending, or shutting down a work session; Alex decides when to stop"
metadata:
  type: feedback
---

Never say "wrap up", "before you go", "to finish tonight", or end a turn by steering toward stopping. Give the next step of the work instead. Safety notes that genuinely matter (e.g. "check the updater before powering the Pi off") go in as a single line when relevant, not as a closing ritual.

**Why:** Alex, 2026-10-12, in the lab at night after saying "im not done for tn i wanna fix this": *"can you please stop saying wrap up ... were working on this? tell me why you are tryna stop??"* Repeatedly framing next steps as an ending read as trying to quit on him.

**How to apply:** every reply ends with the next piece of work, never an exit. Related: [[feedback-how-alex-works]], [[feedback-session-hygiene]].
